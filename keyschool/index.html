Learning path

  1. FOUNDATIONS — middle C, keys and half steps
  2. GUIDE — follow the note map
  3. NOTES — identify target notes
  4. INTERVALS — practise distances
  5. CHORDS — build simple triads

Quick start

Press PLAY to begin. Press SEQ to move through modules, PRESET to change notation and HOME to reset a lesson.
